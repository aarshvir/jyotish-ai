📦 Honest reality

Read once, then stop refreshing the sales number.

$5,000 in week 1 is the stretch, not the plan. At ~$10/sale that's ~500 sales (~70/day) — a brand-new site with no audience and a new ad account can't hit that on paid ads alone. Honest base case: $300–$1,200 week 1. $1,800–$2,500 needs a video to pop. $5k happens only if a video/community post goes viral and you raise average order value (the bundle, Phase 2).

The one most important thing: before spending a rupee or recording a video, fix the funnel‑vs‑product mismatch (Phase 1.5). Your viral plan points strangers at a "free Gun Milan tool" — the free version now exists thanks to the teaser build, but you must point traffic at the right page.

Four things live only in your dashboards (no code can see them): is the Ziina key live? Is OPENAI_API_KEY funded? Is INNGEST_EVENT_KEY set? Are the migrations applied in your live Supabase? Phases 0 & 1 make you prove each by hand.

0.0Find which Supabase project is live 🔴~6 min
Why
Run migrations on the wrong project and they "succeed" but change nothing live — you'd tick the box while every buyer is still locked out.
  1. vercel.com → log in → project jyotish-ai → Settings → Environment Variables.
  2. Click the eye icon on NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL → it reads https://abcd1234.supabase.co → copy the part before .supabase.co. Write it down.
  3. supabase.com → each project → Project Settings → General → Reference ID. The one that matches is your live DB. Bookmark it.
Done when
One project's ref matches. Every Supabase step happens only in this project.
If stuck
No match → you're in the wrong Supabase login. Don't run migrations anywhere until it matches.

Phase 1.5 — The funnel decision 🔴

Before any ad/video/post. It rewrites all your copy.

Your viral plan needs a free thing to point people to. The teaser build now gives /synastry a free 36-point score (breakdown stays paid) and /kundali a free chart summary (full reading paid). Decide where traffic goes and keep "free" honest:

  • Recommended: point matchmaking ads/videos at /synastry — strangers get their free Gun Milan score, then "comment your score" works.
  • Point Kundali ads at /kundali (free chart summary) or the homepage free Kundli.

If you point "free" ads at a page that errors or forces login first, Google disapproves them (₹0 spent) or they convert ~0%. Confirm in incognito what a logged-out stranger sees before spending.

Phase 2 — The offer

⚠ NOT a tonight task — day-2 priority. The only realistic path to $5k. Launch without it; add it day 2.

Bundle = hardcoded price, NOT a coupon. "LAUNCH40" silently does nothing (promos are skipped for standalone products). Hardcode a "Complete Reading" bundle (Kundali + Matchmaking + Monthly Forecast) at $29 / ₹2,499 with a struck-through "$39 / ₹2,999" and "Launch price — ends Sunday." Monday, raise it.

Trust on every product page: the 24-hour money-back guarantee (prominent), a free/redacted sample, a one-line "who made this," and real testimonials once you have buyers.

Capture emails (week-1, highest ROI after the bundle): ~98% of visitors leave forever — add "enter your email to save your score" + a 2-email follow-up.
