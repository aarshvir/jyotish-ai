## Working Strategy (Provisional)

Serve three overlapping groups through one coherent product: people exploring their first Vedic chart; people reflecting on everyday timing; and people exploring compatibility or a specific chart factor. Start with deterministic free chart facts and clear examples. Explain astrology as an interpretive tradition, keep generated commentary distinct from calculation, and reuse entered details only with meaningful user control.
