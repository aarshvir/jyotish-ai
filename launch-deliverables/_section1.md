### 1.3 The category we own

```
AI Vedic astrology — hour-by-hour timing.
```

- **Always pair "AI Vedic astrology" with "hour-by-hour timing."** The first half is the search term people type; the second half is the wedge. Together they are searchable *and* distinctive.

### 1.4 What we sell emotionally (the real purchase)

People buy a feeling and a habit, not "an astrology report." Every piece of copy points quietly at one of these five emotional jobs:

1. **Timing awareness** — knowing *when*, not just *what*. "Before the decision, check the window."
2. **A calmer way to reflect before decisions** — a pause button before you send the message or start something that matters.
3. **Modern Jyotish without the friction** — no phone calls, no waiting rooms, no sending birth details to a random WhatsApp number.
4. **Privacy and control** — a structured, private alternative to ad-hoc astrology.
5. **A daily ritual** — "What are my clearer windows today?" — small, repeatable, sticky.

**Rule of thumb:** sell the *feeling of timing awareness and calm reflection*, never *certainty about the future*. A better lens, not a guarantee.

### 1.5 The three content pillars (the discipline that makes the launch work)

For the launch you need **repetition of a few clear ideas**, not variety. **Everything you publish must fit one of these three pillars.** If a post idea doesn't fit one, don't make it.

**Pillar 1 — "Not another horoscope"** — Generic horoscopes are too vague for real decisions; VedicHour gives specific timing *windows* from your own chart. *Use for:* cold-traffic hooks, the "why we're different" explainer. *Sample line:* "Stop asking 'good day or bad day?' Ask 'which window?'"

**Pillar 2 — "Your day is not one mood"** — Life happens hour by hour; some windows are clearer, some heavier. *Use for:* the product demo, the 18-hours visual, the daily-ritual angle. *Sample line:* "Some hours feel clearer. Some feel heavier. Your day was never one block."

**Pillar 3 — "Private, modern Jyotish"** — Jyotish privately and on your own terms — no calls, no waiting, no vague predictions, no fear. *Use for:* trust-building, the skeptic-friendly angle, the NRI/professional audience. *Sample line:* "Modern Jyotish — no calls, no pressure, no doom. Just a structured second lens."

**The focus discipline (non-negotiable for launch week):** three pillars only; repeat your winning hooks instead of inventing new ones; niche topics (Manglik, Sade Sati, nakshatra) are **SEO/lead-magnet plays** handled by the website's programmatic pages and blog, **not** your daily social rotation; every post ends pointing at one real next step (usually the free preview report).

### 1.6 Brand voice

**Calm, credible, modern, and quietly premium — never spooky, never guru-like, never hypey.** Think "a thoughtful friend who happens to understand both astronomy and Jyotish."

**We sound like this:** calm & grounded (never urgency-panic or doom); plain-spoken (short sentences, explain jargon the moment it appears); credible & specific (name the real mechanism — 18 planetary hours, Swiss Ephemeris, Lahiri ayanamsa); quietly premium (confident, uncluttered); respectful of the tradition.

**We never sound like this:** spooky/fear-mongering ("the stars are warning you…"); guru-like ("only the enlightened understand…"); salesy ("LAST CHANCE!!! transform your destiny NOW!!!"); falsely certain ("you *will* get the job").

| Say this | Not this |
|---|---|
| "a clearer window for focused work" | "a lucky hour to get rich" |
| "a heavier window — maybe slow down" | "a dangerous time, beware" |
| "a structured second lens" | "the absolute truth about your future" |
| "computed with the Swiss Ephemeris" | "ancient secret the universe revealed" |
| "explore your timing privately" | "find out what fate has decided" |

**The read-aloud test:** if a sentence sounds like a calm, intelligent friend explaining something useful, it passes. If it sounds like a late-night infomercial or a horror-movie trailer, rewrite it.

### 1.7 The compliance-safe language framework (one framework for the whole document)

This is the single safety standard every other section inherits. It keeps ad accounts from getting banned and is simply more honest, which sells better. **There are no exceptions.** If a word is on the "avoid" list, it does not get published — not in an ad, caption, DM, or email.

**Core principle:** we talk about **timing windows for reflection and planning** — *clearer* and *heavier* windows. We never talk about **good/bad fate, luck, or guaranteed outcomes.** We offer a *lens*, never a *promise*. No medical, legal, financial, or relationship claims.

**Safe words & phrases (use freely):**
```
clearer window / heavier window · timing awareness · reflection and planning
a personal Vedic timing grid / timing dashboard · a structured second lens
private Jyotish report · hour-by-hour timing · computed with the Swiss Ephemeris + Lahiri ayanamsa
explained in plain English · explore your chart privately
better for focus / better for conversations / better for reflection
```

**Avoid words & phrases (never publish):**
```
guaranteed / guarantee / will definitely · good luck / bad luck / unlucky / lucky hour
fix your life / change your destiny / transform your fate
avoid disaster / danger / doom / curse / dosha will ruin
become rich / get the job / pass the exam / win · save your marriage / your relationship is in danger
cure / heal / treat (anything medical) · predict exactly / know your future for certain
"best hour" / "worst hour"   ← use "clearer"/"heavier" instead
```

> **Note on "best/worst hour":** even though the product *rates* hours, in **public copy** always say **clearer / heavier**, not best/worst. "Best/worst" implies a guarantee of outcome; "clearer/heavier" describes the quality of the window.

**Paid vs. organic separation (critical — they are not the same):** paid ad copy must be stricter than organic. Ad platforms (Meta especially) penalize copy that implies the *viewer personally* has a problem.

| | **Organic** (your feed, posts, DMs) | **Paid ads** (money behind them) |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Educational, warm, can be topical | Extra calm, general, neutral |
| Personal targeting of pain | Never imply hardship; can speak to curiosity/interests | **Never** imply the viewer has a problem or negative status |
| Allowed framing | "Here's how Vedic timing works…" | "Generate your free preview report." |
| Banned | fear, doom, guarantees (always) | **also:** any question implying the viewer's struggle |

**The paid-ad "never imply their pain" rewrites:**

| Never run as an ad | Run this instead |
|---|---|
| "Struggling in your marriage?" | "Explore Vedic compatibility themes privately." |
| "Are you financially stuck?" | "Explore your timing windows for reflection and planning." |
| "Feeling unlucky lately?" | "See your day as a personal Vedic timing grid." |
| "This timing will make you succeed." | "Not another horoscope — a personal Vedic timing grid." |

**Paid-ad targeting rule:** do not target sensitive categories — religion, relationship hardship, negative financial status, health, trauma. Use broad audiences and **search intent** instead (people already searching "free kundli," "kundli matching," "ai astrology"). Safer and cheaper.

### 1.9 The link rule (so the founder can measure what works)

Every public link **must** carry UTM parameters, because the `/admin → Acquisition` dashboard reads `utm_source` on every visit. A link without a UTM is a wasted measurement.

**Format:**
```
https://www.vedichour.com/PAGE?utm_source=CHANNEL&utm_medium=PLACEMENT&utm_campaign=launch
```

**Examples you can copy:**
```
Instagram bio:    https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=launch
YouTube Shorts:   https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=shorts&utm_campaign=launch
Meta ad:          https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=meta_ads&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=launch
Google ad:        https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=google_ads&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=launch
Partner/creator:  https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=partner&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=PARTNERNAME
```

Change `utm_source` to match the channel; keep `utm_campaign=launch` for the launch period. For partners, add `utm_content=THEIRNAME`.
