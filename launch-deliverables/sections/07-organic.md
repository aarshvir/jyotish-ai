## 6. Organic Social Playbook

This is the engine. With a ~$200 total budget, ads alone will not hit launch revenue — organic short-form video is what actually fills the funnel. This section is the standing operating procedure (SOP) for organic: exact account setup per platform, paste-ready captions + hashtag sets, posting cadence, the weekly batch workflow that produces everything in one sitting, and the daily engagement routine that compounds reach.

**Where the actual content lives:** the *volume* — the 30-day caption calendar, the 15 Reel/Short scripts, the 10 carousel outlines, the 15 image-generation prompts, and the Pinterest pins — is all in `launch-deliverables/MARKETING_CONTENT_PACK.md` (sections 1–4 and 9). Do not rewrite that content. This playbook tells you the *system* for shipping it: how to set up, batch, post, link, and engage. When a step says "pull the next script," you are pulling from the content pack.

**The one rule that makes the /admin dashboard work:** every link you ever post — bio links, link stickers, pinned-comment links, YouTube descriptions — MUST carry UTM parameters. The /admin Acquisition tab reads `utm_source` on every visit. A link without UTMs is a visit the founder cannot attribute, which means you cannot tell which platform is making money. No naked `vedichour.com` links. Ever. The exact links are in section 6.4 below — copy them from there.

### 6.3 Captions & hashtags — the paste-ready system

**Do not write captions from scratch.** The 30-day calendar in `MARKETING_CONTENT_PACK.md` section 1 already has 30 paste-ready captions, each with a hook line, full caption, and 15 hashtags. Your job is to copy the right row for the day and adapt the CTA link line. Below are the *reusable building blocks* so every post is consistent and compliant.

#### Caption skeleton (use for any video where the calendar doesn't already give you one)

```
[HOOK LINE — first line, the scroll-stopper, max ~8 words]

[2–4 sentences of plain-English value. Teach one thing. No fear, no guarantees,
no "good/bad fate." Use "clearer / heavier timing windows," not "lucky/unlucky."]

🔭 Pull your free Kundli and see your hours mapped out.
Free at the link in bio · code NEWUSER30 for 30% off your first report.

Your life, decoded hour by hour.
```

#### Compliance guardrails for every caption (non-negotiable)

- ✅ Say: "clearer windows," "heavier windows," "timing awareness," "a window often used for," "traditionally avoided for new starts."
- ❌ Never say: "lucky/unlucky," "good/bad day," "this will make you rich/successful," "avoid disaster," "save your marriage," "guaranteed," anything medical/legal/financial.
- Organic captions can be *educational* and topical (e.g., "Rahu Kaal is the daily window elders avoid for new beginnings"). They still stay calm — never imply the viewer personally has a problem.
- Disclaimer is not needed on every post, but pin one to your IG profile and YouTube About: *"For reflection and planning only. Not medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice."*

#### Hashtag sets (copy-paste blocks)

Instagram/TikTok do best with ~12–15 mixed-size tags. Rotate between these four blocks so you don't get flagged for copy-pasting the identical set every time. The 30-day calendar already gives a tailored 15-tag set per post — use those first; these are fallbacks and for off-calendar posts.

### 6.5 Posting cadence

Consistency beats perfection. The realistic target for one founder, set against the content pack's stated volume (3 short videos + 1 static/carousel per day in the GPT plan; the content pack gives a full 30-day calendar to draw from):

#### Launch week (days 1–7) — heavy

| Slot (your local time) | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Morning (~9–11am) | 1 Reel/Short | IG Reels + YT Shorts + TikTok + FB Reels (same file) |
| Midday (~1–3pm) | 1 Story (poll/question) | Instagram Stories (use a link sticker) |
| Evening (~6–9pm) | 1 Reel/Short + 1 carousel OR static | IG + YT + TikTok + FB; carousel → IG + Pinterest |
| Anytime | 3–5 Pinterest pins | Pinterest |

That's **2–3 videos/day + 1 carousel + 1 Story + pins** — matching the content-pack volume. The best posting windows for this audience skew evening (after-work scroll) and late-morning; if Insights later show a different peak for *your* account, follow the data.

#### Steady state (week 2+) — sustainable

Drop to **1 video/day across all four video placements + 3 carousels/week + daily Story + 3 pins/week.** Quality and engagement matter more than raw count once the launch spike passes.

#### Sequencing logic (which topic, which day)

Rotate the three content pillars from the GPT plan so you never post two of the same angle back-to-back:

1. **"Not another horoscope"** (differentiator/AI/Swiss Ephemeris)
2. **"Your day is not one mood"** (the 18 planetary hours / timing)
3. **"Private modern Jyotish"** (free tools, calm/skeptic-friendly, education)

The 30-day calendar already interleaves these — just follow it top to bottom. Push the free-kundli link as the default CTA; switch the IG bio top-link to *pricing* only on the 1–2 days you run an explicit offer post.

### 6.6 The weekly batch workflow (one ~3-hour session = a full week of posts)

Batching is the only way one person sustains this. Do it once a week (e.g., Sunday). Every step names the tool, the prompt, and where the output goes.

**Outcome of one session:** 7 vertical videos (each → 4 placements), 3 carousels, ~10 pins, captions written and queued. Folder structure mirrors the GPT plan's Drive setup (`02 AI Images`, `04 CapCut Videos`, `03 Canva Posts`).

#### Step 1 — Write/confirm the week's captions & scripts (20 min)

- Open `MARKETING_CONTENT_PACK.md`. Pick the next 7 calendar rows (videos) + the next 3 carousel outlines.
- For any gap, open **ChatGPT** (you own Plus) and paste the master content prompt from the GPT plan (section 10) to generate fresh hooks/scripts. Then run them through the compliance guardrails in 6.3.
- Paste each caption + its 15 hashtags into a simple doc, one per planned post, with the correct UTM CTA line. This is your "script of record" for the week.

#### Step 2 — Generate the backgrounds/visuals (30 min)

- Open **Gemini** (you own Advanced — best owned image tool). For each video, paste the matching image prompt from `MARKETING_CONTENT_PACK.md` section 3 (15 ready prompts), or the reusable 9:16 background prompt from the GPT plan section 12.1:
  ```
  Create a vertical 9:16 premium VedicHour background. Deep navy near-black (#0a0a1a) background, thin gold (#d4af37) circular timing grid, subtle star field, faint amber glow, minimal, premium, calm, modern AI Jyotish aesthetic. No text, no people, no gods, no temples, no zodiac clichés. 1080x1920.
  ```
- Download each PNG → save to the `02 AI Images` folder. You want ~7 backgrounds.
- For carousels, use **Canva** (Canva Pro recommended buy in the GPT plan, ~$13/mo — worth it for resizing + brand kit). Build a reusable 1080×1080 template once with the brand colors (navy `#0a0a1a`, gold `#d4af37`, Cormorant Garamond headline / DM Sans body), then duplicate it and drop in each carousel's slide copy from section 4 of the content pack.

#### Step 3 — Assemble the 7 videos (60–75 min)

- Open **CapCut** (free; CapCut Pro optional). For each video: New project → 9:16 → import the Gemini background → add the script as large on-screen captions (CapCut auto-captions if you record a voiceover; otherwise type the on-screen beats from the script) → add a text-to-speech voiceover *or* leave it text-only with trending audio → add the end card:
  ```
  Try your free Kundli
  VedicHour.com · NEWUSER30
  ```
- **Voiceover direction** (if using TTS): *"Calm Indian English, premium, warm, credible — not spooky, not guru-like, not salesy."* If CapCut's TTS sounds robotic, ElevenLabs Starter (~$5) is the optional upgrade — only if needed.
- Export each as MP4 → save to `04 CapCut Videos`. Keep the first frame clean and bold (it becomes the cover).

#### Step 4 — Caption the covers / thumbnails (15 min)

- The Reel cover text matters more than the video for the IG grid. In CapCut or Canva, set a 1-line cover hook (the script's hook line) on the first frame. For YouTube Shorts, a vertical thumbnail isn't required but a strong first frame helps.

#### Step 5 — Schedule everything (30 min)

- **Instagram + Facebook:** use **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com → Planner) — it's free and schedules Reels to both IG and FB at once. Upload the 7 videos, paste captions + hashtags, set the slots from the cadence table.
- **YouTube Shorts:** YouTube Studio → upload each MP4, title with hook + `#shorts`, paste the YouTube Shorts UTM link on line 1 of the description. Schedule the publish times.
- **TikTok:** TikTok's web uploader (tiktok.com/upload) lets you schedule up to 10 days out. Upload the same MP4s, paste caption + hashtags.
- **Pinterest:** Pinterest's native scheduler lets you schedule pins. Upload the pin images (carousel slides re-exported as 1000×1500, or the static AI images), paste titles/descriptions from content-pack section 9, set the destination to the Pinterest UTM link.
- **Stories** can't all be pre-scheduled natively — post those live daily (they're 30 seconds of work each).

#### Step 6 — Log it (10 min)

- Open the `Content Tracker` tab (columns defined in the GPT plan Part 3.2). For each scheduled post add: Date, Platform, Asset Type, Hook, UTM Link, Pillar. After it's live you'll fill Views/Clicks/Leads from each platform's analytics + the /admin Acquisition tab. This is how you learn which hooks to repeat.

**That's the week.** Daily effort drops to: post the live Stories, and do engagement (next section).

### 6.7 Daily engagement tactics (30–45 min/day — this is where reach is won)

The algorithm rewards *conversation*, not just posting. A post with 20 thoughtful comment-replies in the first hour will out-travel an identical post you ignored. Daily routine:

#### The first-60-minutes protocol (per post)

1. **Reply to every comment** within the first hour, especially with a question back ("What's your Moon sign? I'll tell you your nakshatra trait" — this is literally built into several calendar posts). Replies = comments = ranking signal.
2. **Pin your best comment** containing the free-kundli UTM link (use the IG Reel UTM link). Link-in-comment is how you get a clickable link on a Reel without sending people to the bio.
3. **Heart + reply to the first ~10 comments** to seed momentum.

#### Daily outbound engagement (the "give-to-get" loop)

- **Comment genuinely on 10–15 posts/day** from adjacent accounts: bigger Vedic astrology creators, nakshatra/dasha pages, spirituality accounts, wedding/matchmaking pages. Add real value (answer a question, add a fact) — not "great post 🔥". This puts @vedichour in front of *their* audience and often earns a profile visit. This doubles as soft partner-warming for the outreach motion.
- **Answer Stories questions** in your niche; reshare relevant ones with a thoughtful add.
- **Use the calendar's interactive Stories** (polls: "Mars hora vs Venus hora," question stickers: "Drop your Moon sign") — interaction stickers massively boost Story reach and feed DMs you can convert.

#### DM handling (warm leads land here)

- When someone DMs asking about their chart, reply helpfully with one genuine insight, then: *"You can pull your full free Kundli here 👉 [IG Story UTM link] — and code NEWUSER30 takes 30% off the deeper reports if you want them."* Helpful first, link second.
- Save good exchanges as anonymized testimonials (ask permission before reposting).

#### Comment-to-content loop

- Every "great question" in your comments is your next Reel. Screenshot it, answer it on camera/text. This is the cheapest, highest-performing content source — real audience questions out-perform invented hooks.

#### Cross-pollination

- Repost your best-performing Reel as a Story the next day with a "ICYMI" sticker + link sticker.
- Drop your strongest carousel into relevant **Facebook groups** (caption + value, link only if the group allows it — the content pack section 6 has 10 group-safe posts written for this).

#### What NOT to do (account-safety)

- Don't follow/unfollow in bulk, don't comment the identical line on 50 posts, don't buy followers — these get accounts throttled or banned and the GPT plan flags them explicitly.
- Don't paste the exact same caption + hashtag block on every single post (rotate the four hashtag blocks in 6.3).

### 6.8 Weekly review (15 min, every Sunday before the next batch)

Open each platform's analytics + the **/admin Acquisition tab** and answer three questions, logging to the Content Tracker:

1. **Which `utm_source` drove the most free-Kundli starts and the most paid sales?** Acquisition tab shows this directly. Put more batch effort into that platform next week.
2. **Which hook/pillar got the highest watch-time and saves?** Make 3 variants of the single best hook (the GPT plan's "repeat the winning hook" rule). Saves and shares matter more than likes for this niche.
3. **Which posts got clicks but no leads** (people clicked the link but didn't start a Kundli)? That's a landing-page or offer-clarity issue — flag it; the CTA copy may need work.

**Keep / Kill / Scale** every angle using the GPT plan's rules: scale what gets leads + checkout starts + genuine questions and is policy-safe; kill what gets confused comments, clicks-without-leads, or feels fear-based. Within ~2 weeks you'll know whether IG Reels or YouTube Shorts is your money channel, and you pour the batch hours there.
