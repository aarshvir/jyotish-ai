## 11. 7-Day Launch Calendar, Daily 60-min Workflow & KPIs

This is the section you live inside for launch week. It tells you exactly what to do each day, the single 60-minute routine you repeat every single day, the numbers to watch on your own `/admin` dashboard, and the rules for when to kill something or pour more money into it. Nothing here requires you to write or design anything by hand — every text/image/video is generated by an AI tool with a copy-paste prompt, and every link is a tracked UTM link so your `/admin` Acquisition tab can tell you which channel actually pays.

**Launch-week revenue goal:** $2,000. **Stretch:** $5,000 (only if one video breaks out or a partner pushes real traffic). **Operating budget:** ~$200 external spend, mostly ads, started small and scaled only onto proven winners.

**Before Day 0, confirm these are true (these are the only things you, the human, must verify once):**
- The site is live at `https://www.vedichour.com` and the free preview report generates.
- `/admin` loads and you can see the tabs: Acquisition, Revenue, Retention, Call-list, Ops.
- Coupon `NEWUSER30` works at checkout (test it once with a real cart — apply the code, confirm 30% comes off, then abandon).
- Lifecycle emails are live (report-ready, welcome, abandoned-checkout) — they already are via Resend, so you do not build them.

| Lead → buyer conversion | Revenue purchases ÷ Acquisition leads | 10%+ | 8-12% | Under 3% (offer/page problem, not traffic) |

### 11.6 The North-Star & the One Diagnostic Number

Your single North-Star metric is **Revenue in the `/admin` Revenue tab**. But revenue is a lagging number — by the time it's low it's too late to react. So each day you also watch one leading diagnostic:

**Lead → buyer conversion rate** (Revenue purchases ÷ Acquisition free-Kundli starts).

- If **leads are high but conversion is low** → the traffic is fine; the problem is the offer or the checkout. Action: lead with NEWUSER30 in copy, push the $9.99 Deep Kundli (low-ticket first purchase), and check Ops for checkout errors. Do **not** buy more traffic.
- If **conversion is fine but leads are low** → the product converts; you just need more top-of-funnel. Action: post more, and scale the winning ad.
- If **both are low** → it's a message/positioning problem. Action: stop spending, re-read your hooks against the safe-language rules, and lean harder on the one organic hook getting the most comments.

This one branch tells you whether to spend on traffic, fix the funnel, or fix the message — without guessing.

### 11.7 Kill / Scale Decision Rules

Run these against the `/admin` numbers, not against your feelings about a post.

**KILL a creative or ad if ANY of these is true:**
1. It gets clicks (Acquisition shows visits) but **zero free-Kundli leads** after 24h.
2. Its **cost per lead is over ~$5** with no purchases (Ad spend ÷ its leads).
3. Watch time is low / comments are confused ("what is this?").
4. It reads fear-based, exaggerated, or implies the viewer's hardship — kill on sight, it's an ad-policy risk.
5. It pulls only freebie-hunters and no buyers after Day 4.

> To kill: in Meta/Google Ads set the ad's status to Off. In your Content Tracker mark it `KILL`. Do not delete — keep it as a record of what didn't work.

**SCALE (raise budget / make more variants) only if ALL of these are true:**
1. It produces **free-Kundli leads** (Acquisition).
2. It produces **at least one purchase or checkout start** (Revenue).
3. It's policy-safe (no fear, no guarantees, no personal-hardship targeting).
4. It matches the positioning ("Your Life, Decoded Hour by Hour" / "not another horoscope").
5. Its **cost per lead is under ~$2** (for paid).

> To scale: raise that one ad's daily budget by ~50-100% (e.g. $10 → $20), and make 3-5 new variations of its hook. **Scale the winner, never the average.** Never raise the whole campaign budget — only the proven ad inside it.

**The $200 budget guardrail:** start ads at $10/day each (Meta + Google). Only increase spend onto an ad that has already produced a purchase. If by Day 4 **no** ad has produced a purchase, cut paid ads to $5/day and put your energy into organic + outreach (which cost $0 and, at this scale, usually drive more of the revenue anyway). Never let total ad spend cross ~$150 in launch week unless you are clearly profitable on a channel.

### 11.9 If You Only Have 20 Minutes (the bad-day fallback)

Some days you will not have an hour. On those days, do this and nothing else — it protects the funnel:
```text
[ ] Re-post yesterday's best video to all 4 platforms (no new creation)  — 5 min
[ ] Reply to every comment/DM                                            — 5 min
[ ] Send 5 warm follow-ups from the Contact Tracker                      — 5 min
[ ] Open /admin: check Ops (errors?) + Revenue (any sale?), fill the row — 5 min
```
Never skip the `/admin` check — a broken report or a paused-by-accident ad costs more than a missed post.
