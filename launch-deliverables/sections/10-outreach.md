## 9. Outreach & Partnerships (your first sales team)

No small-budget launch survives on ads alone. With ~$200 to spend, the math is brutal: ads get you a *few* dozen clicks, but outreach gets you warm, trusting buyers for $0. This section is your sales team. You do not write a single message by hand — AI writes everything, you copy-paste, you log it in one sheet, you follow up. That's the whole job.

> **The one rule that makes this work:** every link you ever send a human carries a UTM tag, and every partner gets their own coupon code. That way your **/admin → Acquisition** tab shows you exactly which outreach motion (and which partner) drove a *paying* customer — so by Day 7 you stop guessing and double down on what works.

---

### 9.1 The Contact Operating System — 4 motions

You run four parallel "motions." Each is a different kind of person, a different script, and a different goal. Do not mix them up.

| # | Motion | Who | Goal | Tool to find them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Warm** | Friends, family, alumni, ex-colleagues, your WhatsApp contacts | Honest feedback → free Kundli → first sales + testimonials | Your own phone/WhatsApp/Instagram |
| 2 | **Professional / LinkedIn** | Founders, NRIs, consultants, thoughtful professionals you're connected to | Credibility, "is this idea clear?", word-of-mouth | LinkedIn "My Network" |
| 3 | **Creator / Partner** | Astrology creators, wellness coaches, wedding planners, matchmakers, newsletter & podcast hosts | Recurring traffic via their audience + revenue share | Instagram search + Apollo.io (motion 3b below) |
| 4 | **Lead follow-up** | People who generated a free Kundli but didn't buy | Convert free → paid with the deeper reports | Your **/admin → Call-list (CRM)** tab + lifecycle emails |

**Why these four, in this order:** Warm contacts give you your first dollars and your first testimonials *today* (they trust you). Professionals give you credibility and reach. Creators/partners are the only motion that scales beyond your own network. Lead follow-up is the cheapest revenue of all — these people already raised their hand.

> **Motion 3b — Apollo.io (the systematic partner engine).** Once you've messaged the creators you can find by hand, use **Apollo.io** (free tier) to find hundreds more at once. Full setup is in §9.8. Treat Apollo as the *scaled* version of motion 3 — same offer, same scripts, just more reach.

### 9.2 Daily & weekly targets

Pace matters. Sending 200 identical DMs in an hour gets your account flagged as spam and can get you restricted. Spread it out, personalize lightly, and hit these numbers.

**Daily (about 45 minutes, split morning/evening):**

```text
[ ] Add 20 new contacts to the tracker
[ ] Send 15 warm/professional first messages
[ ] Send 5 creator/partner DMs (or 25/day in an Apollo sequence)
[ ] Send follow-ups to everyone who didn't reply 2 days ago
[ ] Reply to EVERY response (same day)
[ ] Move any buyer to the Customer tab + send the feedback request
[ ] Update each row's status
```

**Weekly launch-week targets:**

| Motion | Week target | Daily target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Warm personal contacts | 100 | 15 |
| LinkedIn / professional | 100 | 15 |
| Creators / partners | 100 (incl. Apollo) | 15 |
| Communities / groups | 20 | 3 |
| Free-Kundli lead follow-ups | all leads | daily |

**What success looks like by Day 7:**

| Metric | Target |
|---|---:|
| Total replies | 50+ |
| Free Kundli trials from outreach | 100+ |
| Sales from outreach | 20+ |
| Revenue from outreach | $1,000+ |
| Partner replies | 15+ |
| Partner posts / collabs live | 3–5 |

> Do not panic if replies are slow on Day 1–2. Outreach compounds: the follow-up (message 2) often out-converts the first message. The system only fails if you stop following up.

### 9.6 Revenue share / affiliate via UTM + per-partner coupon

You don't need affiliate software. You already have everything: the **/admin Acquisition tab reads `utm_source` on every visit**, and **/admin → Coupons** lets you create a code per partner. Together they ARE your affiliate system.

**The two-key attribution method (use both per partner):**

1. **A unique UTM link** — so you see their *traffic* and trials in /admin → Acquisition.
2. **A unique coupon code** — so you see their *paid* conversions in /admin → Revenue, even when someone buys days later on a different device (the code follows them, the cookie may not).

**Revenue-share offer (only promise what you can track and pay):**
```text
20% of net revenue on any purchase attributed to your link or code during launch week.
```

**How you actually pay it (manual, fine for launch):** at week's end, open **/admin → Revenue**, filter by each partner's coupon code, sum the net revenue, multiply by 20%, and pay via the method you agreed (UPI/PayPal/Wise). Log the payout in the partner's tracker row. Done.

> Keep it honest: only offer revenue share to partners whose code/link you've actually created. Never promise a share you can't attribute — it's the fastest way to burn a partner relationship.

### 9.11 Outreach safety rules (read before you send anything)

1. Don't spam groups. Value first; link only when it genuinely helps the thread.
2. Don't mass-DM identical text fast — personalize lightly and stay under the daily caps.
3. Never pretend you know someone's personal problem ("struggling in your marriage?"). Say "explore compatibility themes privately" instead.
4. No guarantees, no fear, no medical / legal / financial / relationship claims — in DMs just as in ads.
5. Don't ask for a testimonial before they've actually used the product.
6. Never post someone's birth details, chart, or report screenshots publicly.
7. Don't buy fake reviews or fake followers — ever.
8. Don't promise revenue share you can't attribute and pay.
9. Keep paid-ad copy stricter than organic DMs; never imply the reader's personal hardship.
10. Only advertise coupons that exist: `NEWUSER30` (public) and a partner's own code. `ADMIN100` is owner-only — never share it.
