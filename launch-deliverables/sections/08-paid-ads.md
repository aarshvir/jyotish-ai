## 7. Paid Ads (Google + Meta) within budget

> **Read this first.** Paid ads are NOT your main growth engine — your free tools, content, and outreach are. Ads are a *measurement instrument*: a small, controlled spend that tells you which message and which product people actually pay for, so you know what to scale organically. You start at **$10/day total** and you never increase spend until the `/admin` dashboard shows a real signal. Everything here is copy-paste. Every word of ad copy is already written. Every link already carries the UTM tags that light up your `/admin → Acquisition` tab.
>
> **Your total external budget is ~$200.** This section spends **$0** on tools (Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager are free to use — you only pay for the clicks) and recommends a **$140 test budget** over 14 days, leaving ~$60 as a reserve to pour into whichever single ad wins. Do not spend the reserve until you have a winner.

---

### 7.0 The one rule that keeps you out of trouble

Astrology is a **"sensitive" / restricted category** on both Google and Meta. Ads that imply you know the viewer's personal misfortune ("Are you unlucky in love?", "Is Sade Sati ruining your life?") get **rejected, or worse, get your whole ad account banned** — and a banned account is very hard to get back. So we follow the GPT plan's safety framework exactly:

- **Sell the tool, not a fear.** "Generate your free Kundli" — not "Fix your bad luck."
- **No guarantees, no medical/legal/financial/relationship claims.** Say "clearer and heavier timing windows for reflection and planning," never "good days / bad fate / save your marriage / become rich."
- **Never imply you know the viewer's pain.** "Explore Vedic compatibility themes privately" — not "Are you struggling in marriage?"
- **Put the disclaimer in the ad where it fits.** "For reflection and planning only. Not medical, legal, or financial advice."
- **Do NOT target by religion, relationship status, health, or financial hardship.** Use broad audiences (Meta) and search intent (Google) instead. This is both a policy rule and a performance rule — broad just works better now.

Two copy banks live in this section: **AD-SAFE copy** (this is what you paste into the ad platforms) and a short **BANNED list** (phrases to never use). When in doubt, use only the exact strings provided here.

### 7.5 How to read results in `/admin` + the ad dashboards

You have **three** screens. Read them in this order, once a day, for 5 minutes.

**1) `/admin → Acquisition` (the truth source — does this channel make money?)**
- This tab buckets every visit by `utm_source`. You'll see rows like `google`, `meta`, plus your organic sources.
- The only number that matters: **paying customers (and revenue) per source.** A channel with 500 visits and 0 paid customers is losing; a channel with 60 visits and 4 paid customers is your winner — even if it looks "smaller."
- Cross-check the **Revenue** tab for which *product* sold (Deep Kundli vs Matching vs a subscription) and the **Retention** tab to see if buyers come back. Scale the source AND the product that the dashboard rewards.

**2) Google Ads dashboard (is the click-buying efficient?)**
- Watch: **Clicks**, **CTR** (aim **>3%** on Search; under ~1.5% means the copy is weak), **Avg. CPC** (you capped it at $0.50), and **Conversions** once tracking is on.
- The **Search terms** report (7.3.4) is your cleanup tool.

**3) Meta Ads dashboard (which creative is cheap to click?)**
- Watch: **CPC / Cost per link click** (good is roughly **$0.20–$0.60**; over ~$1 = kill it), **CTR (link)** (>1% is healthy), and **Cost per result.**
- Compare your 3 Reels-as-ads; one will be clearly cheaper. That Reel is also your best **organic** content — make more like it.

> **The decision loop, in one line:** `/admin` tells you which *channel + product* earns money → the ad dashboards tell you which *keyword/creative* is cheap → you kill the rest and put the reserve behind the one thing that wins on both.

### 7.6 Kill / keep / scale rules (decide on day 11, then weekly)

**KILL immediately if any of these are true:**
1. **0 leads/sales after $25 spent** on that ad group/creative.
2. **Lots of clicks, zero free-Kundli starts** (the click is curious but the page/offer mismatched — pause and rethink the landing page, don't keep paying).
3. **CTR below the floor** (Google Search <1.5%, Meta link <1%) after ~50 clicks.
4. **Meta CPC over ~$1** after $20.
5. The ad got **disapproved twice** — stop, rewrite to the AD-SAFE bank, don't risk the account.

**KEEP (let it keep running at $10/day) if:**
- It's producing free-Kundli starts at a sane cost and at least breaking even, but isn't a runaway winner yet. Give it more days before judging.

**SCALE (move the ~$60–$90 reserve here, raise to $15–20/day) only if ALL are true:**
1. It produced **actual purchases** (seen in `/admin → Revenue`), not just clicks.
2. Cost per purchase is **below the product price** (e.g. a $9.99 Deep Kundli sale that cost <$9.99 in ads — ideally well under, to leave margin).
3. It's **policy-safe** and on-brand ("Your life, decoded hour by hour").
4. When you scale, **raise budget by ~20–30% every 2–3 days, never double it overnight** — sudden jumps reset the platform's learning and spike your cost-per-result.

> **Final beginner guardrail:** if after the full $140 test you have *no* clear winner, **stop paid ads** and put 100% of your energy into free content + outreach (the other sections). Paid ads are optional fuel; they are never the engine. Re-test paid later, with a sharper free-Kundli offer, once organic is producing buyers.
