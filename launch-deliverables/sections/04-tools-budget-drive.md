## 3. Tool Stack, Budget & Google Drive HQ

This section gives you the **smallest possible set of tools** to run the entire launch, an honest **$200 budget plan** (what to buy, what to skip, and why), and a **Google Drive + Sheets headquarters** so nothing gets lost. You already own the three most important tools (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude). The rule never changes: **AI makes everything, you only copy-paste, click, and upload.**

> One sentence to remember: *Owned AI writes and draws everything → Canva assembles it → CapCut animates it → you schedule it → /admin tells you what worked.*

---

### 3.1 The recommended stack (best-in-class, unbiased)

These are the genuinely best tools for each job at your scale — not the most expensive, not vendor-loyal. **Eight tools run the whole launch.** Anything not on this list, you do not need in week one.

| Job | Best tool | Cost | Why this one (unbiased) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Words: captions, scripts, ad copy, outreach, editing | **Claude** (you own it) + **ChatGPT Plus** (you own it) | $0 (owned) | Claude is the strongest long-form writer/editor; ChatGPT is great for fast variations. Use whichever you have open. |
| Images: backgrounds, logos, post art | **Google Gemini (Advanced)** (you own it) | $0 (owned) | Nano-Banana / Imagen in Gemini is currently the best text-to-image for clean, text-free cosmic backgrounds. |
| Short video (5–8s cosmic clips) | **Google Veo, inside Gemini** (you own it) | $0 (owned) | You already pay for it. No need for Sora/Runway/Kling/Pika in week one. |
| Design assembly (posts, carousels, resizing) | **Canva Pro** | ~$13/mo (BUY) | The single best assembly station; Magic Resize turns 1 post into 8 sizes. Works on free tier too. |
| Video editing (captions, timing, export) | **CapCut** | $0 free tier (USE) | Best free auto-caption + text-on-screen editor. You almost never need CapCut Pro. |
| Scheduling Instagram + Facebook | **Meta Business Suite** | $0 (free) | Official, free, schedules both at once. |
| Scheduling YouTube Shorts | **YouTube Studio** | $0 (free) | Upload + schedule Shorts directly. |
| Tracking everything | **Google Sheets + your own /admin** | $0 (owned) | Your `/admin → Acquisition` tab already reads `utm_source` on every visit. Sheets is your manual layer on top. |

**That's the whole stack.** Total mandatory new spend so far: **$13/mo for Canva Pro.** Everything else is owned or free.

> **Why no Buffer / Linktree / Apollo / Figma / Notion in week one?** Meta Business Suite + YouTube Studio cover scheduling for free. Your site already has product pages, so you link directly (better for ads than a Linktree). Outreach is done by hand from a Google Sheet in week one — Apollo is a later upgrade once you know it pays back. Figma is overkill; Canva does everything you need. Adding tools is the #1 way beginners freeze. Resist it.

### 3.2 The $200 budget allocation (what to buy vs. skip)

You have ~$200. The biggest lever is **ad spend to test which channel produces paying customers** — but only *after* you have organic posts live. Here is the exact allocation, with an honest worth-it verdict for each line.

| **Figma / Notion / paid Linktree** | $0–$15 | **SKIP** | **Not needed.** Canva + Google Sheets + direct site links cover all of it. |

**Total committed: ~$143 (Canva $13 + Google Ads $70 + Meta Ads $60).** Plus **$45 contingency held** for the winner = **~$188.** That leaves a small buffer and keeps you under $200 with room for one $5 ElevenLabs month if you want it.

> **The one rule that protects your money:** *Do not spend a second dollar on ads until at least one organic post or one ad shows real traction (people clicking through and starting a free Kundli).* Your `/admin → Acquisition` tab shows you this. Scale the winner; kill the rest.

### 3.3 Exact signup + first steps for each paid/free tool

Each one is: **go here → do this → done.** Nothing here requires skill.

**Canva Pro (~$13/mo) — your assembly station**
1. Go to **https://www.canva.com** → click **Sign up** → choose **Continue with Google** (use your VedicHour email).
2. Click your name (top-right) → **Settings → Billing & plans → Try Canva Pro**. Take the free trial if offered, then it becomes ~$13/mo.
3. Left sidebar → **Brand** → **Brand Kit** → add: Colors (paste `#0A0A1A`, `#D4AF37`, `#F7F0DF`, `#FFFFFF`), Fonts (Cormorant Garamond for headlines, DM Sans for body), and upload your AI logo from Drive folder `01_Brand`.
4. You're done. Every future post: search a template → drag in your Gemini image → paste caption → **Share → Download**.

**CapCut (free) — your video editor**
1. Go to **https://www.capcut.com** → **Download** the desktop app (more stable than web for export) → install → sign in with Google.
2. First test: **New project** → **Import** your Gemini background → drag it to the timeline → stretch it to 15 seconds.
3. Add captions: top menu **Text → Auto captions** (if you added a voiceover) or **Text → Add text** for on-screen lines.
4. Export: top-right **Export** → set **1080×1920 (9:16)** → **Export**. Save the MP4 to Drive folder `06_Videos`.
5. **Do not buy CapCut Pro** unless you specifically need a Pro-only effect. The free tier covers captions, text, and HD export.

**Google Ads (~$70 budget) — highest-intent traffic**
1. Go to **https://ads.google.com** → **Start now** → sign in with your Google account.
2. If it pushes you into "Smart" mode, click **Switch to Expert Mode** (small link at the bottom) so you control the budget.
3. Create a **Search** campaign → goal **Website traffic** → set budget **$10/day** → locations: India, UAE, US, UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore → language English.
4. Paste keywords and ad copy from your ads section. **Landing page must include a UTM:** `https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=google_ads&utm_medium=search&utm_campaign=launch`.
5. Launch. Check `/admin → Acquisition` daily to see if `google_ads` is producing signups.

**Meta Business Suite + Meta Ads (~$60 budget) — scheduling + cheap reach**
1. Go to **https://business.facebook.com** → log in with the Facebook account that owns your VedicHour page.
2. **Scheduling (free):** left menu **Content → Create post / Create reel** → upload → **Schedule** → pick day/time. This posts to Instagram + Facebook together.
3. **Ads:** left menu **Ads → Create** → objective **Traffic** → budget **$10/day** → audience age 24–50, your launch countries, English → upload your best organic Reel as the creative.
4. **Destination URL with UTM:** `https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=meta_ads&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=launch`.

**ElevenLabs (optional, ~$5) — only if CapCut's voice sounds bad**
1. Go to **https://elevenlabs.io** → **Sign up** with Google.
2. Pick a calm voice (search "warm, narration"). Paste your script → **Generate** → **Download** the MP3.
3. Drop the MP3 into your CapCut timeline as the voiceover. Save to Drive folder `07_Voiceovers`.

**Gemini / ChatGPT / Claude (already owned) — your content engine**
- **Gemini:** https://gemini.google.com — paste image prompts here, download PNGs. For video, type a Veo prompt and download the clip.
- **ChatGPT:** https://chat.openai.com — fast caption/ad variations.
- **Claude:** https://claude.ai — best for long-form scripts, careful editing, and compliance review ("rewrite this to remove any fear-based or guaranteed-outcome language").

### 3.4 Your Google Drive HQ — exact folder structure

Create this once. Every asset has one obvious home, so you never lose a winning video or re-make an image.

1. Go to **https://drive.google.com** → **New → Folder** → name it exactly:

```
VedicHour Launch HQ
```

2. Open it. Inside, create these subfolders (New → Folder, one at a time):

```
VedicHour Launch HQ/
├── 00_Playbook            ← this master playbook + the canonical facts
├── 01_Brand               ← logo, banner, OG image, color/font notes
├── 02_Prompts             ← every AI prompt you reuse (image, video, copy)
├── 03_Images              ← Gemini PNG backgrounds & post art
├── 04_Canva_Posts         ← exported static posts & carousels
├── 05_Carousels           ← multi-slide source files
├── 06_Videos              ← CapCut MP4 exports (Reels/Shorts)
├── 07_Voiceovers          ← ElevenLabs / CapCut audio
├── 08_Ad_Creatives        ← the exact files you upload to Google/Meta ads
├── 09_Captions_Copy       ← caption text, hashtags, ad copy, email/DM copy
├── 10_Outreach            ← contact lists, partner asset packs
├── 11_Daily_Reports       ← end-of-day screenshots of /admin + Sheets
├── 12_Winners             ← only the assets that actually drove signups/sales
└── 13_Archive             ← everything retired, so the active folders stay clean
```

3. Drag your existing brand files into `01_Brand`. Your site already has assets at `/brand/logo-square.svg`, `/brand/social-bg-square.svg`, and `/brand/social-bg-story.svg` — download those and drop them in `01_Brand` so you never re-make them.

> **The `12_Winners` rule:** the moment any video or image produces real signups (you'll see it in `/admin → Acquisition`), copy it into `12_Winners`. Next week you make variations of *only* what's in this folder. This is how a beginner compounds results without guessing.

### 3.6 The "AI makes everything" 4-step assembly line

Every single post — static, carousel, or video — is built the same way. Memorize these four steps and you never need to think about the process again.

```
1. WORDS    → Claude or ChatGPT writes the caption/script.
              Paste the master content prompt → copy the output → save to Drive 09_Captions_Copy.
2. PICTURE  → Gemini draws the background/art (or Veo makes a 5–8s clip).
              Paste the image prompt → download the PNG/MP4 → save to Drive 03_Images or 06_Videos.
3. ASSEMBLE → Canva (static/carousel) or CapCut (video) combines words + picture.
              Drag in the image → paste the text → export → save to Drive 04_Canva_Posts / 06_Videos.
4. SHIP     → Upload with a UTM link. Schedule in Meta Suite + YouTube Studio.
              Log it in the Content Tracker. Done.
```

**Step 1 — the master prompt to get the words.** Paste this into **Claude** or **ChatGPT** any time you need fresh copy:

> *You are VedicHour's senior direct-response marketer. VedicHour is an AI Vedic astrology platform whose differentiator is rating all 18 planetary hours (horas) of your day — computed with the Swiss Ephemeris + Lahiri ayanamsa — and explaining them in plain English. Tagline: "Your Life, Decoded Hour by Hour." Ad-safe line: "Not another horoscope — a personal Vedic timing grid." Real products: free preview report (one per user), 7-Day Forecast $9.99, Monthly Oracle $19.99, Annual Oracle $49.99, Deep Kundli $9.99, Kundli Matchmaking $9.99. Real coupon: NEWUSER30 = 30% off first paid report. Rules: no fear, no guarantees, no medical/legal/financial/relationship claims; say "clearer / heavier timing windows," never "good/bad fate." Write [X captions / Y reel scripts] for [topic]. Each: a scroll-stopping first line, 3–4 lines of genuine value, a CTA to the free Kundli, and 15 relevant hashtags. End each with "Code NEWUSER30 = 30% off your first paid report."*

**Step 2 — the master image prompt.** Paste into **Gemini** (reply "make it more minimal / move the glow / remove any text" until it's clean):

> *Create a vertical 9:16 premium background for VedicHour. Deep navy near-black (#0A0A1A) background, a thin gold (#D4AF37) circular timing grid, a subtle star field, a faint amber glow. Minimal, premium, calm, modern AI-Jyotish aesthetic. Center kept dark and empty for a text overlay. No words, no people, no gods, no temples, no zodiac clip-art, no hands. 1080×1920.*

**Step 3 — assemble (no skill required).**
- *Static/carousel:* Canva → search "Instagram Post" or "Instagram Carousel" → drag your Gemini image onto a slide → paste the caption text → **Share → Download**.
- *Video:* CapCut → **New project** → import the Gemini background → add on-screen text or an ElevenLabs/CapCut voiceover → **Text → Auto captions** → **Export at 1080×1920**.

**Step 4 — ship + log.** Upload to Instagram (via Meta Suite) and YouTube (re-upload the exact same vertical video as a Short — zero extra work). Put the UTM link in the caption/bio. Add one row to the **Content Tracker**. That's the entire loop.

> **Bottom line:** Three tools you already own (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude) generate 100% of the words and images. One $13 purchase (Canva Pro) and one free tool (CapCut) assemble them. ~$130 in ad tests + a $45 reserve tells you — through `/admin` and your Sheet — exactly which channel to scale. You never write, draw, film, or design anything by hand. You paste, click, schedule, and track.
