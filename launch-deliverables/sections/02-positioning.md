### 1.4 What we sell emotionally (the real purchase)

People do not buy "an astrology report." They buy a feeling and a habit. Every piece of copy should be quietly pointed at one of these five emotional jobs. When a post is performing, it is almost always because it nailed one of these — not because of clever words.

1. **Timing awareness** — the quiet confidence of knowing *when*, not just *what*. "Before the decision, check the window."
2. **A calmer way to reflect before decisions** — a pause button before you send the message, schedule the call, or start something that matters.
3. **Modern Jyotish without the friction** — the wisdom of Vedic astrology without phone calls, waiting rooms, or sending birth details to a random WhatsApp number.
4. **Privacy and control** — a structured, private alternative to ad-hoc astrology. You explore on your own, on your own screen.
5. **A daily ritual** — "What are my clearer windows today?" — a small, repeatable moment that makes the product sticky and makes subscriptions make sense.

**Rule of thumb:** sell the *feeling of timing awareness and calm reflection*, never *certainty about the future*. We give a better lens, not a guarantee. This framing is also what keeps us compliant (see 1.7).

### 1.5 The three content pillars (this is the discipline that makes the launch work)

The biggest mistake the original plan made was scattering across 30 topics (nakshatra, dasha, Manglik, Sade Sati, Rahu Kaal, Gun Milan, Brahma Muhurat…). For the launch you need **repetition of a few clear ideas**, not variety. **Everything you publish must fit one of these three pillars.** If a post idea doesn't fit one, don't make it.

#### Pillar 1 — "Not another horoscope"
- **Core message:** Generic horoscopes are too vague for real decisions. VedicHour gives you specific timing *windows* from your own chart.
- **Use it for:** cold-traffic hooks, the contrast story, the "why we're different" explainer.
- **Sample line:** *"Stop asking 'good day or bad day?' Ask 'which window?'"*

#### Pillar 2 — "Your day is not one mood"
- **Core message:** Life happens hour by hour. Some windows are clearer, some heavier, some better for focus, some for conversations. VedicHour maps them.
- **Use it for:** the product demo, the 18-hours visual, the daily-ritual angle.
- **Sample line:** *"Some hours feel clearer. Some feel heavier. Your day was never one block."*

#### Pillar 3 — "Private, modern Jyotish"
- **Core message:** Use Jyotish privately and on your own terms — no phone calls, no waiting, no vague predictions, no fear.
- **Use it for:** trust-building, the skeptic-friendly angle, the NRI/professional audience, the "what VedicHour is *not*" content.
- **Sample line:** *"Modern Jyotish — no calls, no pressure, no doom. Just a structured second lens."*

**The focus discipline (adopted from the GPT plan — this is non-negotiable for launch week):**
- Three pillars only. Rotate through them; do not add a fourth.
- Repeat your winning hooks instead of inventing new ones. Repetition builds recognition; novelty burns effort.
- Free tools and niche topics (Manglik, Sade Sati, nakshatra, etc.) are **SEO and lead-magnet plays**, handled by the website's programmatic pages and blog — **not** your daily social rotation. Keep social on the three pillars.
- Every post must end by pointing at one real next step (the free preview report, almost always).

### 1.6 Brand voice (how everything should sound)

The voice is the reconciliation of both plans: **calm, credible, modern, and quietly premium — never spooky, never guru-like, never hypey.** Think "a thoughtful friend who happens to understand both astronomy and Jyotish," not "a mystic warning you about your fate."

**We sound like this:**
- **Calm & grounded.** Lower the temperature. We never use urgency-panic or doom.
- **Plain-spoken.** Short sentences. Explain jargon the moment it appears, or don't use it. "Plain English" is a product feature *and* a voice rule.
- **Credible & specific.** We name the real mechanism — 18 planetary hours, Swiss Ephemeris, Lahiri ayanamsa — because specifics build trust. Vagueness is the enemy.
- **Quietly premium.** Confident, uncluttered, never desperate. We don't beg; we invite.
- **Respectful of the tradition.** Jyotish is treated with respect, not as a gimmick or a meme.

**We never sound like this:**
- Spooky, ominous, or fear-mongering ("the stars are warning you…").
- Guru-like or superior ("only the enlightened understand…").
- Salesy or hypey ("LAST CHANCE!!! transform your destiny NOW!!!").
- Falsely certain ("you *will* get the job," "this *guarantees*…").

**Voice quick-reference (say / don't say):**

| Say this | Not this |
|---|---|
| "a clearer window for focused work" | "a lucky hour to get rich" |
| "a heavier window — maybe slow down" | "a dangerous time, beware" |
| "a structured second lens" | "the absolute truth about your future" |
| "computed with the Swiss Ephemeris" | "ancient secret the universe revealed" |
| "explore your timing privately" | "find out what fate has decided" |

**The em-dash test for voice:** read any sentence aloud. If it sounds like a calm, intelligent friend explaining something useful, it passes. If it sounds like a late-night infomercial or a horror-movie trailer, rewrite it.

### 1.7 The compliance-safe language framework (one framework for the whole document)

This is the single safety standard every other section inherits. It exists for two reasons: (1) it keeps ad accounts from getting banned, and (2) it is simply more honest and more trustworthy, which sells better anyway. **There are no exceptions. If a word is on the "avoid" list, it does not get published — not in an ad, not in a caption, not in a DM, not in an email.**

#### The core principle
We talk about **timing windows for reflection and planning** — *clearer* and *heavier* windows. We never talk about **good/bad fate, luck, or guaranteed outcomes.** We offer a *lens*, never a *promise*. We never make medical, legal, financial, or relationship claims.

#### Safe words & phrases (use freely)

```
clearer window / heavier window
timing awareness
reflection and planning
a personal Vedic timing grid / timing dashboard
a structured second lens
private Jyotish report
hour-by-hour timing
computed with the Swiss Ephemeris + Lahiri ayanamsa
explained in plain English
explore your chart privately
better for focus / better for conversations / better for reflection
```

#### Avoid words & phrases (never publish)

```
guaranteed / guarantee / will definitely
good luck / bad luck / unlucky / lucky hour
fix your life / change your destiny / transform your fate
avoid disaster / danger / doom / curse / dosha will ruin
become rich / get the job / pass the exam / win
save your marriage / your relationship is in danger
cure / heal / treat (anything medical)
predict exactly / know your future for certain
"best hour" / "worst hour"   ← use "clearer"/"heavier" instead
```

> Note on "best/worst hour": even though the product *rates* hours, in **public copy** always say **clearer / heavier**, not best / worst. "Best/worst" implies a guarantee of outcome; "clearer/heavier" describes the quality of the window for reflection. This is a deliberate, important distinction — keep it.

#### Paid vs. organic separation (critical — they are not the same)

Both source plans agree on this and it is one of the most important safety rules: **paid ad copy must be stricter than organic copy.** Ad platforms (Meta especially) penalize copy that implies the *viewer personally* has a problem. Organic posts can be educational and topical; ads must be calm, general, and never personally targeted.

| | **Organic** (your own feed, posts, videos, DMs) | **Paid ads** (Meta, Google — money behind them) |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Educational, warm, can be topical | Extra calm, general, neutral |
| Personal targeting of pain | Never imply hardship, but you *can* speak to curiosity and interests | **Never** imply the viewer has a problem, hardship, or negative status |
| Allowed framing | "Here's how Vedic timing works…" / "Your day is not one mood" | "Generate your free preview report." / "See your personal Vedic timing grid." |
| Banned in this column | fear, doom, guarantees (always) | **also banned:** any question that implies the viewer's struggle |

**The paid-ad "never imply their pain" rule, with rewrites:**

| ❌ Never run as an ad | ✅ Run this instead |
|---|---|
| "Struggling in your marriage?" | "Explore Vedic compatibility themes privately." |
| "Are you financially stuck?" | "Explore your timing windows for reflection and planning." |
| "Feeling unlucky lately?" | "See your day as a personal Vedic timing grid." |
| "Is your relationship in danger?" | "A private, plain-English look at compatibility themes." |
| "This timing will make you succeed." | "Not another horoscope — a personal Vedic timing grid." |

**Paid-ad targeting rule:** do not target sensitive categories — religion, relationship hardship, negative financial status, health problems, or trauma. Use broad audiences and **search intent** instead (people already searching "free kundli," "kundli matching," "ai astrology"). This is both safer and cheaper.

#### The standard disclaimer (use everywhere)

Put this in the website footer, in paid landing pages, in ad descriptions where space allows, and at the bottom of the lifecycle emails:

```
For reflection and planning only. Not medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice.
```

### 1.9 The link rule (so the founder can measure what works)

Every public link **must** carry UTM parameters, because the `/admin → Acquisition` dashboard reads `utm_source` on every visit — that is how you learn which channel actually drives paying customers. A link without a UTM is a wasted measurement.

**Format:**

```
https://www.vedichour.com/PAGE?utm_source=CHANNEL&utm_medium=PLACEMENT&utm_campaign=launch
```

**Examples you can copy:**

```
Instagram bio:    https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=launch
YouTube Shorts:   https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=shorts&utm_campaign=launch
Meta ad:          https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=meta_ads&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=launch
Google ad:        https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=google_ads&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=launch
Partner/creator:  https://www.vedichour.com/free-kundli?utm_source=partner&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=PARTNERNAME
```

Change `utm_source` to match the channel; keep `utm_campaign=launch` for the launch period so everything rolls up together. For partners, add `utm_content=THEIRNAME` so you can credit them.
