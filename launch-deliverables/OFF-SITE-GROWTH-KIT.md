## 6. "Keep It Substantiable" Caution Box

> ⚠️ **Before you amplify any public claim off-site, make sure it's independently verifiable.** Reviewers, journalists, Product Hunt moderators, and AI answer engines now actively fact-check product claims. An unverifiable boast costs you credibility and can get a listing rejected or a post removed.
>
> **Do not publish off-site until you can prove it:**
> - **User / download counts** — only if your analytics back the exact number on the day you claim it.
> - **Star ratings** — only if a real, public review profile shows it. Never seed or buy reviews.
> - **"Featured in" / press logos** — only with a real, linkable article.
> - **Success-rate or accuracy %** — avoid entirely. It conflicts with the brand promise (awareness, not certainty) and can't be substantiated for astrology.
> - **Awards / "#1" / "best"** — only if a named, datable source awarded it.
>
> **Safe to lead with anytime** (product facts, not claims): Swiss Ephemeris + Lahiri ayanamsa · all 18 horas rated clearer/heavier · plain-English AI report · free Kundli with no card · one-time reports, no subscription · 36-point Gun Milan + 8 calculators.
>
> Rule of thumb: *if a skeptical journalist asked "show me," could you?* If not, build the proof first, then claim it.
