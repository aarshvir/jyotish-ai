## PART 1 — The weekly content machine (30 min/week → 7 posts)

Do this every Sunday. It produces the week's content with zero writing.

### 1.1 Get the words (2 min)
Open `MARKETING_CONTENT_PACK.md` → "30-Day Content Calendar." Copy this week's 7 captions + hashtags into a note.

### 1.2 Get the images (10 min)
For each of the 7 posts, open **Gemini**, paste the matching image prompt from the pack's "AI Image Prompts" section, download the 7 PNGs.

### 1.3 If you want NEW words (any time), paste this into ChatGPT or Claude:
> "You are VedicHour's social media manager. VedicHour is an AI Vedic astrology app (free Kundli + hour-by-hour forecast, Swiss Ephemeris precision, tagline 'Your life, decoded hour by hour'). ### 1.4 Make them into posts (10 min)
Open Canva → search "Instagram Reel" or "Instagram Post" template → drop in your image → paste the caption text → export. (Or skip Canva entirely: upload the Gemini image straight to Instagram and paste the caption.)

### 1.5 Schedule the week (8 min)
- **Instagram + Facebook:** Meta Business Suite → "Create post"/"Create reel" → upload → "Schedule" → pick the day.
- **Everything else:** Buffer → connect accounts → queue the same posts.

> **Video Reels, monkey-proof:** the highest-performing astrology content is a still cosmic image + text-on-screen + trending audio. In Canva: pick an "Instagram Reel" template → swap in your Gemini image → paste the script's on-screen text (from the pack) → click any trending audio → Export. No filming, no face. If you want motion, Gemini's video (Veo) or ChatGPT's Sora can generate a 5-second cosmic clip from a prompt in the pack — download it and use it as the background in Canva.
