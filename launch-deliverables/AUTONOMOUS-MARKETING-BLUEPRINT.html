01The honest verdict first

You can get to roughly 85% autonomous and safely "check once a week." You cannot get to 100% lights-out — and you shouldn't want to. The ~15% you deliberately keep (approving ad creative, signing off spend, and consenting outreach) is exactly the slice where account bans and legal liability are born. Designed correctly, your weekly touch is about 30 minutes.

Runs itself

Content & organic

Daily blog, daily reels/posters from your scripts, scheduled multi-platform publishing, hashtags, SEO, KPI dashboards, lifecycle email to opted-in users.

Auto + weekly gate

Paid & outreach

Ads run and auto-optimize against rules, but you approve new creatives and watch a hard spend cap. Cold email runs capped on warmed domains. WhatsApp only to opt-ins.

Never automate

The red lines

Bot likes/follows/DMs, cold SMS, cold WhatsApp blasts, and any "guaranteed / miracle" ad claim. These are ban-and-lawsuit territory.

The one-sentence strategy: a single "Marketing Brain" (an orchestrator + Claude) directs nine specialist agent-loops that read your Content Pack, manufacture and publish collateral, run and tune ads, capture every visitor, and feed all results back into a shared memory that decides what to make more of — with a thin human approval gate on the two things that can hurt you.

02The mental model

Think of it as a small autonomous marketing agency that never sleeps: a manager (the Brain), nine specialists (the loops), a shared filing cabinet (Memory/Data), a switchboard to every tool (Integration), and a Monday-morning review desk (the Cockpit). Everything an "employee" does that spends money or contacts a stranger passes across the review desk first.

03The five layers, concretely

1 · The Brain  orchestrator

n8n (self-hosted) on a $6–12/mo droplet as the spine — it has scheduled triggers, ~70 AI nodes, native human-approval steps, and speaks MCP both ways. Claude is the reasoning layer it calls to plan, write, judge and decide. This is the "manager" that runs the daily plan and routes work.

2 · The Agent-Loops  the workers

Nine repeatable workflows (Section 04). Each is a small pipeline: trigger → gather → Claude reasons → tool acts → log result → maybe ask a human. They run on schedules (daily/hourly) or on events (a new lead, an ad crossing a threshold).

3 · Integration  the switchboard

Where a tool has a mature MCP server (ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Apollo, Klaviyo/HubSpot, PostHog, Google Ads) Claude drives it directly. Everything else is a REST node inside n8n. One rule: nothing reaches a platform except through an official API.

4 · Memory & Data  the filing cabinet

A content library (every script/asset + its status), a performance warehouse (every post/ad + its metrics), an attribution table (which creative drove which sale), the brand kit, and a consent log for outreach. This is what makes the system learn rather than just do.

5 · The Human Cockpit  your 30 min/week

One dashboard + one approval queue. You approve the week's new ad creatives, glance at the spend cap and KPI trend, clear any flagged items, and (optionally) thumbs-up next week's content themes. Everything else already happened. A single kill-switch pauses all spend instantly.

04The nine autonomous loops

Each loop maps directly to something you asked for. AUTO runs unattended; HITL has a human gate on the risky step.

L1

Daily Blog & SEO content

AUTO
Trigger: daily 6am. Does: picks the next topic from an SEO keyword backlog (Search Console "striking-distance" queries + your Content Pack), Claude drafts a 1,200–1,800-word article grounded in your own blog style, generates a hero image (Ideogram/Flux), publishes to the blog with schema + internal links, pings Google for indexing.
Tools: GA4 + Search Console API → Claude → Ideogram → your CMS/Next.js content API → IndexNow.
Decision rule: prioritise keywords ranking #5–15 (fastest wins). Guardrail: brand-voice + claims check before publish (no medical/financial promises).
L2

Short-form video factory (reels / shorts / loops)

AUTO
Trigger: daily, batched. Does: pulls 3–4 approved scripts from the Content Pack/library → ElevenLabs voiceover → Veo 3.1 (vertical b-roll) or HeyGen avatar → auto-captions + your brand background → renders 9:16 MP4s → stores in the library marked "ready".
Tools: Content library → ElevenLabs (MCP) → Veo via fal.ai / HeyGen (MCP) → captions → render → storage.
Decision rule: make more of whatever format L6 says is winning. Guardrail: don't build on Sora (its API closes Sept 2026); cap render spend/day.
L3

Publish & distribute

AUTO
Trigger: when assets are "ready" + an optimal slot. Does: posts the day's reels to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook, (TikTok once audited), writes platform-specific captions + hashtag sets, adds the tracked bio/landing link, schedules across the week.
Tools: Postiz (self-host, free) → graduate to Ayrshare; native YouTube/Instagram Graph/TikTok APIs.
Hard limits to respect: IG ~100 API posts/24h; YouTube ~6 uploads/day (quota); TikTok needs app audit (else private-only). Guardrail: stay well under caps; multi-week app-review lead time — keep a manual fallback at launch.
L4

Technical SEO & programmatic pages

AUTO
Trigger: weekly + on publish. Does: monitors Search Console for crawl/index issues, refreshes the sitemap, auto-builds programmatic pages (per-nakshatra, per-sign, per-dosha) from your engine data, fixes internal-link gaps, tracks ranking movement.
Tools: Search Console API, GA4, your chart engine, Ahrefs/DataForSEO (optional).
Guardrail: rate-limit page generation so Google sees quality, not a content dump.
L5

Paid ads — create, monitor, pause, scale

HITL on creative
Trigger: continuous monitor (hourly) + weekly new-creative batch. Does: builds Google Search + Meta/Instagram campaigns from your winning organic hooks, attaches tracked landing links (UTM), then runs rules: pause an ad/keyword if CPA > target or CTR < floor after enough data; shift budget to winners (≤20%/change); flag fatigue → request fresh creative from L2/L9.
Tools: Google Ads API + Meta Marketing API; Revealbot for rule execution.
The gate: a human approves each new ad creative + copy (policy risk) and the system obeys a hard daily spend cap. Auto-bidding and pause/scale need no approval. Guardrail: no "guaranteed/miracle" copy; astrology framed as guidance + disclaimer.
L6

The Optimization Brain (the feedback loop)

AUTO
Trigger: nightly. Does: pulls every metric (views, CTR, sessions, sign-ups, sales, CPA, ROAS) into the warehouse, attributes sales back to creatives/keywords, then decides: scale winners, kill losers, tell L9 which themes to make more of and which to retire, and write the weekly cockpit summary. This is the part that "monitors the platform and tweaks everything."
Tools: GA4 + PostHog (MCP) + ad APIs → warehouse → Claude → Looker Studio dashboard.
Guardrail: needs ~3–7 days of data before acting on a verdict (avoids over-reacting to noise).
L7

Lead-gen & cold email (B2B: astrologers, wedding planners, affiliates)

capped
Trigger: daily, throttled. Does: finds relevant partners/affiliates on Apollo, enriches, Claude personalises a short pitch, sends via warmed inboxes, auto-follows-up, books replies into the CRM.
Tools: Apollo (MCP) → Clay enrich → Smartlead (warmup + send) → Brevo/HubSpot CRM.
Hard rules: CAN-SPAM compliant (physical address + 1-click unsubscribe); ≤40 emails/mailbox/day on domains warmed 4–6 weeks; auto-suppress unsubscribes + Germany. Note: this is B2B partner outreach — your D2C buyers come from L1–L5, not cold email.
L8

Lifecycle email & WhatsApp (opt-in only)

AUTO to opt-ins
Trigger: event-based. Does: the free-Kundli visitor gives email/WhatsApp → automated welcome + value sequence → cart-abandon nudge → post-purchase upsell to the other two products → weekly "your timing this week" broadcast. This is your biggest revenue multiplier.
Tools: Brevo (email) + WhatsApp Business Cloud API (approved templates).
Red line: no cold WhatsApp blasts and no cold SMS — instant ban / TCPA exposure. WhatsApp marketing only to users who opted in, with a logged consent timestamp and the 24-hour window respected.
L9

Creative Director — judge existing, regenerate new

AUTO
Trigger: weekly + on L6 signal. Does: reads the entire script/content library, scores each asset against actual performance, marks the good ones "reuse/remix", and for tired or thin themes writes brand-new scripts and image/video prompts for L2 to produce. This is the "if the content is good use it; if not, generate new prompts/scripts" engine.
Tools: Content library + L6 performance data → Claude (ideation + scriptwriting).
Guardrail: new scripts pass the same brand-voice/claims check before they enter the L2 queue.

06"Connect to every tool" — the integration reality

You can connect to almost anything, in one of three ways, in this order of preference:

  • MCP-native (best): Claude controls the tool directly with no glue code. Already mature for ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Recraft, Apollo, Clay, Klaviyo, HubSpot, PostHog, Buffer, Google Ads, and n8n itself.
  • Official REST API wrapped in n8n (most things): video/image generators, the social publishing APIs, Meta ads, WhatsApp Cloud, Brevo, Smartlead, GA4. Reliable and ToS-safe — this is the bulk of the build.
  • Browser automation (last resort, fragile): only for tools with no API. Avoid for posting/engagement — it violates platform ToS and gets accounts banned.
"Every tool in the world" has a ceiling, and it's not technical — it's policy. The platforms deliberately cap automation: Instagram allows ~100 API posts/24h, TikTok forces unaudited apps to private-only, YouTube ~6 uploads/day. So "connect everything" really means "connect everything through official, rate-limited, ToS-compliant doors" — which is plenty for your scale, but not infinite.

08The weekly cockpit — your 30 minutes

Everything converges into one page and one queue. A realistic Monday ritual:

  1. Glance at the scoreboard (5 min): sessions, sign-ups, sales, CPA, ROAS vs last week — auto-built in Looker Studio by L6.
  2. Clear the approval queue (15 min): thumbs-up/down next week's ad creatives and any flagged outreach. Rejections send notes back to L9 for a rewrite.
  3. Check the guardrail panel (5 min): spend-cap usage, WhatsApp quality rating, email spam-rate, any platform warnings.
  4. Optional steer (5 min): tell the Brain a theme to push or avoid this week (e.g., "more matchmaking, it's wedding season").
Kill-switch: one toggle pauses all paid spend and outbound sending immediately — your circuit-breaker if anything looks wrong while you're away.
